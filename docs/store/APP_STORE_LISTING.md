## Description

Welcome to the Festival Plaza — a cozy Día de los Muertos village you can poke, drag, and fling like a pocket toy box.

**Living cast**  
Invite Pepita, Abuela Rosa, Xolo, Gato, and the full festival crew. They wander, skip, wave, smell flowers, and rest on benches while you play.

**Toys**  
Wind gusts, marigold rain, mariachi music, and pan dulce treats. Toggle day and night with the sun/moon button.

**Missions that loop**  
Two chore sets — welcome and festival — guide you from ofrenda marigolds to candle paths, stage encores, and tree offerings.

**Made for curious fingers**  
No fail state. Pick anyone up. Explore buildings. Watch for shooting stars and parade drums in the distance.
